Notes for Tomas

Ideas from Dad

This page is for you, not for customers. It lists what this version adds to your BuildIT site, and why. The same notes are in the README. Take the bits you like.

What changed, and why

Tabs, one job per page

Home, Why I'm doing this, Services, Work, Pricing, How it works, About, FAQ and Contact. Your live site is one long page, which is a good start. Separate pages let someone open Pricing or Work directly, and they show the shape of the business at a glance. Why I'm doing this is its own tab, placed next to Home. Social media marketing is not its own tab. It lives on Services and Pricing, which are already in the menu.

Your copy stays

BuildIT branding, Ember & Oat, Ironbark Electrical, Lux-Lashes (lux-lashes.com), and Innovo (innovotech.com) are yours. The email on the site is now tomas@buildit.net.au. The phone number on every page, the contact buttons, and the search listing is 0403 993 242. The dollar amounts are the same: $1,250, $1,800 up front, and $99 a month. Hosting is no longer bundled into the $1,250. The plan names changed. See the notes at the bottom.

Perfect Jobs & Adventures (perfectjobsandadventures.github.io) is a real site you built. It is first under Current work on the home page and the Work page, tagged Live site, ahead of the example designs. The card image is a screenshot of the live homepage, saved at assets/work/perfectjobs.png. The link opens in a new tab. The one-line description is what the site is. No stats, results or testimonials were added.

Pricing comparison table

The table now has four columns: Design & build, Design & build + Hosting & monitoring, Hosting & monitoring only, and Social media marketing. Included and not included are written in words. Prices that are not set say Placeholder. On a phone, swipe sideways. The first column stays put.

About you, and the Northern Beaches

Local customers want to know who they are emailing. The About page is in your voice: you name yourself, you say BuildIT is your studio, and you say why you are doing this. It stays on Sydney's Northern Beaches. I didn't invent suburbs, a work history, client numbers or awards.

Contact and brief forms email you directly

Both forms post to FormSubmit, which emails tomas@buildit.net.au. There is no Netlify Forms step and no dashboard to open. The contact subject is "BuildIT website enquiry". The brief subject is "BuildIT design brief". After a send, the page goes to thanks.html. The mailto links stay on the pages as a fallback. The first real submission asks FormSubmit to send you one activation email.

A next step on every page

Each page ends with contact, call, or both. People decide on the page they are already reading. They shouldn't have to hunt for a button.

Mobile menu and contrast

From 1180px wide and down, the links sit in the menu button, and the open menu scrolls. Between 1181px and 1500px the desktop links use a slightly tighter gap. Your dark colours already clear WCAG AA for normal text. Unset prices are the word Placeholder, not colour alone. The table still says "Included" and "Not included" in words.

Share preview image

Every public page has Open Graph and Twitter card tags pointing at your existing og-image.png, with width, height and alt text. Links in messages and social apps then show the BuildIT card instead of a blank preview.

Nothing made up

No testimonials, review scores, "trusted by" counts or awards. The two prices that were never given still say Placeholder. The why-copy is a draft in your voice, from the reasons below. It is not a made-up life story.

All In look, and the cursor trail

The public pages use large type, a cyan band of business types, glass cards, and pricing levels. Level 1 is design and build at $1,250. Level 2 is design and build plus hosting and monitoring at $1,800 up front, then $99 a month. Level 3 is social media marketing, and the price says Ask me. Social is in the menu, on the home page, on Services, and on Pricing. The cursor trail stays. The trail is off when someone asks for reduced motion. A mouse move draws it on the empty background. A touch pointer does not. A finger drag still draws while it is over the background. The trail stays off text, cards, and the levels. Contact opens with the phone, the email, and a compact form. The form card stays about 520px wide.

Why I'm doing this

The home page, at the bottom, and its own nav tab now say why you are doing this, in the first person, as something you are doing now. The draft says: not long ago you were making choices you are not proud of, and you hurt people. You are changing that, right now. This business is how you are doing it, one honest job at a time. The story page values are honesty (the real price and timeline, no surprises), owning it (if you get something wrong, you fix it), and showing up (you do what you say, when you say). No events, numbers or quotes were added.

Edit this copy into your own words.

Hosting is optional

Design & build stays $1,250 one-time, and hosting is not in it. Domain setup stayed on that price, because your live site already included the domain at $1,250. Move the domain if you want it elsewhere. Design & build + Hosting & monitoring stays $1,800 up front, then $99 a month. The extras that were on Website + Care at that price are still listed there: contact button, Google setup, Maps, speed, accessibility, visitor stats, monthly update, backups, security updates and email support. They were not renamed into "monitoring" without being listed. Hosting & monitoring only has no price. The card and the table say Placeholder. Backups, security updates and email support on hosting-only also say Placeholder, because those were part of the $1,800 plan, not a separate hosting-only list.

Social media marketing

A new service: post building, content creation, and active sales and marketing via socials. It is on Services, on Pricing, on the home page as a price card, and in the contact form. There is no separate tab. The price says Ask me. Networks and how many posts are not set. No results, follower counts or ad spend were invented. The public pages do not ask you to fill those in. Set the price when you have one.

Page limits

Design and build at $1,250, ex GST, is up to 5 pages and 3 revisions. Design and build plus hosting and monitoring at $1,800 up front, then $99 a month, ex GST, is up to 8 pages, a basic SEO set-up (titles, descriptions, a Google Business link, and analytics), hosting and monitoring, and 3 revisions. Extra pages are $150 per page, ex GST, on either paid plan. That is on the home price cards, the Pricing levels, the paid plan cards, the comparison table, Services, and the paid FAQ answers. The FAQ question is "What if I need more pages?" The old line about tweaking until someone is happy now says 3 revisions.

One assurance line

There was no contracts page and no contract file to delete. The contract sections, the contact card, the price-side contract lines, and the FAQ question "Who is the contract with?" are gone. The only line left is in the footer: "BuildIT is backed by Nick Finlayson, and the work is contracted through Innovo." Innovo still links to innovotech.com. The Work page still says you built Perfect Jobs & Adventures, and that Nick Finlayson built the Lux-Lashes and Innovo sites.

Email

The address is tomas@buildit.net.au. It is on the contact blocks, the email line at the bottom of each page, the mailto links, the FormSubmit form action, the example sites, and the search listing. The public canonical URL is still the GitHub Pages address. No CNAME is set. The live site is buildit.net.au.

External site links

Every link to innovotech.com and lux-lashes.com opens in a new tab, including the Innovo link in the footer on every page. The Work cards for Lux-Lashes and Innovo already did. No script opens those sites.

Prices are ex GST

Every price on the Pricing page is ex GST. It is in the hero, above the four offers, on the comparison headings, in the table caption, and in the FAQ. The home price section says the same, so those figures are not read as GST-inclusive. No GST amount was calculated on top. The $1,250, $1,800 and $99 figures are unchanged.

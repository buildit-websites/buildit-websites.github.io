People

Six things, all at once

People are the first element. A good match is six things together, not one impressive line on a page.

Right skills
What someone can actually do, shown in the work, not only claimed.
Right role
The work as it really is. The title is the least interesting part.
Right team
Who they will sit with, rely on, and hear the truth from.
Right fit
How they think, decide, and disagree.
Right expectations
Said in plain language, on both sides, before anyone commits.
Right aspirations
Where they want the work to take them, and whether this is that path.

Miss one of the six and the rest strains. Khemistry looks at all of them, then moves quickly. The standard is the same in an early-career conversation and in a conversation with the person who leads an organisation.

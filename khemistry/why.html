Why Khemistry

Three lines. They are the whole method.

    We don’t advertise. We work with people we know, people they know, and through carefully curated direct approaches.

Where the work sits

Khemistry was built from conversations that became genuine connections and long-term partnerships. Maidlin Mistry has spent more than 25 years in this work, across Australia, South East Asia and the UK. The manner is direct and honest.

Most conversations are with superannuation funds and investment teams. The rest are with people who have known Maidlin for years, across manufacturing, purpose-led organisations, logistics and wholesale.

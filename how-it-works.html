How it works

From first email to a live site

  1. 01

    You get in touch

    Tell me about your business, what you'd like customers to know, and any sites you like the look of. If you have a logo or photos, send them too. If not, that's fine.

  2. 02

    I design and build it

    I put together your site and send you a preview link. You tell me what to change. The price includes 3 revisions.

  3. 03

    I launch it, or hand it over

    If hosting is part of the job, the site goes live at your web address over HTTPS. If you only want design and build, I hand the site over so you can host it where you choose. Hosting is not included in design and build.

  4. 04

    I keep it running, if you added hosting

    With Hosting & monitoring, I keep the site online. The $1,400 plan is up to 8 pages and includes one update a month. Design and build alone is up to 5 pages, and hosting stays with you.

What to have ready

Just the basics: what your business does, how customers can reach you, and your opening hours or service area if you have them. A logo and photos help, but if you don't have them, let me know and I'll talk through the options.

How long it takes depends on the size of your site and how quickly I can get your details and photos. Start your project and I'll give you an idea of timing before I start.

After launch, it depends on hosting. With Hosting & monitoring, the site stays online with me. On design and build alone, hosting is not included and the finished site is yours to host.

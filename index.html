What you get

Everything your website needs, in one place

Most small businesses don't want to deal with web designers, domain companies and hosting providers all at once. Design and build come from me. Hosting and monitoring are optional. Built for local businesses on Sydney's Northern Beaches.

A website built for your business

A modern site designed around what you do, with your words, your photos and your colours. It works on phones, tablets and computers, and it is built for HTTPS, so visitors see the padlock once the site is hosted.

Your own domain

A proper web address for your business, with your choice of a .com or a .com.au. I help you pick it, register it and connect it to your site, so customers can find you and you look established from the start.

Hosting, if you want it

Hosting is optional. If you add it, I set up the hosting and the HTTPS certificate, so the site stays up without you logging in to a control panel. Design and build does not include hosting.

I look after it

On Design & build + Hosting & monitoring I keep looking after the site after launch: backups, security updates, one update a month, and help by email when you need a change.

How it works

Four simple steps

  1. 01

    You get in touch

    Tell me about your business, what you'd like customers to know, and any sites you like the look of. If you have a logo or photos, send them too. If not, that's fine.

  2. 02

    I design and build it

    I put together your site and send you a preview link. You tell me what to change. The price includes 3 revisions.

  3. 03

    I launch it on your domain

    Once you give the go-ahead, and if hosting is part of the job, the site goes live at your web address over HTTPS. If you only want design and build, I hand the site over so you can host it where you choose.

  4. 04

    I keep it running, if you add hosting

    Hosting is optional. If you add Hosting & monitoring, I keep the site online. On design and build alone, hosting is not included, and the site is handed over for you to host.

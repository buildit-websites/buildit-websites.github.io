FAQ

Questions we get asked

What's included in the price?

Both plans include a custom website built for your business, HTTPS security, your domain set up and connected, and hosting set up for you. Website + Care adds everything in the list above, including monthly updates, domain and hosting renewals, backups, security updates and support by email.

What's a monthly update?

Each month we make one upgrade to your site, like adding new info, refreshing the design or adding photos.

Do I pay for my domain or hosting each year?

On Website + Care, no, renewals are included in your one-time price. On the Website plan, renewals after the first year aren't included, so email us and we'll explain the options.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of your site and how quickly we can get your details and photos. Email us about your business and we'll give you an idea of timing before we start.

What do I need to give you?

Just the basics: what your business does, how customers can reach you, and your opening hours or service area if you have them. A logo and photos help, but if you don't have them, let us know and we'll talk through the options.

What happens after my site launches?

Your site stays online on the hosting we set up. On Website + Care we keep looking after it: monthly updates, backups, security updates and email support when you need a change. On the Website plan, your site is finished and live. If you'd like changes down the track, email us and we'll let you know the options.

Is my site secure?

Yes. Every site we build is served over HTTPS, the same secure connection banks and online shops use, so visitors see the padlock in their browser.

Are the example sites real businesses?

No. Ember & Oat and Ironbark Electrical are made-up businesses we built to show the kind of sites we make. Your site will be designed around your own business.

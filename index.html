CH.02 — Then

Where I started

For a while I lost my way. I fell in with the wrong crowd, and I went along with things I’m not proud of. I hurt people along the way, including the people closest to me.

I’m not going to dress that up or ask you to feel sorry for me. It happened, and it’s mine to own.

CH.03 — The turn

But that isn’t who I want to be, and it isn’t who I’m going to be.

So I’m changing what I do every day. I get up on time, I show up, and I tell the truth even when it’s hard. When I get something wrong, I fix it instead of blaming someone else.

Trust is something I have to earn back, one day and one promise at a time.

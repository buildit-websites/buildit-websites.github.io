What you get

Aimed at local businesses on Sydney's Northern Beaches. The website and the domain come with design and build. Hosting is a choice.

A website built for your business

A modern site designed around what you do, with your words, your photos and your colours. It works on phones, tablets and computers, and it is built for HTTPS, so visitors see the padlock once the site is hosted.

Your own domain

A proper web address for your business, with your choice of a .com or a .com.au. I help you pick it, register it and connect it to your site, so customers can find you and you look established from the start.

Hosting, if you want it

Hosting is optional. If you add Hosting & monitoring, I set up the hosting and the HTTPS certificate, so the site stays up without you logging in to a control panel. The $1,250 design and build price is up to 5 pages, and it does not include hosting.

I look after it

On Design & build + Hosting & monitoring I keep looking after the site after launch: backups, security updates, one update a month, and help by email when you need a change.

About

I'm a Northern Beaches web designer

I'm Tomas. BuildIT is my studio for local businesses on Sydney's Northern Beaches. I design and build the website and set up the domain. Hosting and monitoring are optional, so you can send customers a link you're proud of either way.

Who you'll be talking to

Not long ago I was making choices I'm not proud of. I'm changing that, right now. This business is how I'm doing it, one honest job at a time.

My core values are what I'm doing now, every day: honesty, owning it, giving back, earning trust, and showing up. The longer version is on Why I'm doing this.

You don't need to know anything about websites. I explain things in plain English and handle the technical side: HTTPS, and your choice of a .com or a .com.au. Hosting is optional.

BuildIT is backed by the experience of Nick Finlayson, founder and CEO of Innovo, who built the Lux-Lashes and Innovo sites on the Work page. Lux-Lashes is a mobile lash artist on Sydney's Northern Beaches. Innovo is at innovotech.com (opens in a new tab).

About · Northern Beaches

Hi, I'm Tomas. This is BuildIT.

I'm 15, I live on Sydney's Northern Beaches, and BuildIT is my studio for local businesses. I design and build the website and set up the domain. Hosting and monitoring are optional, so you can send customers a link you're proud of either way.

Why I'm doing this

Not long ago I was with the wrong crowd. I made bad choices, and I hurt people, including my family. I'm saying that straight. I'm not asking you to feel sorry for me.

I'm choosing better choices and better behaviour. Doing what's right, every day. BuildIT is how. A clear price, work I can put my name on, and doing what I said I would do.

I'm flipping my life around through this business, and by learning properly in the AI industry. That is the work now. One honest job at a time.

The first five websites are free, in exchange for an honest review. Charity and community sites are free on an ongoing basis, separate from those five. I took from a community once. Putting something useful back is part of earning trust I have not been given yet.
